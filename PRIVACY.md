# Privacy Policy: Liminal

Liminal does not collect or share personal data. The app has no accounts,
analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party services. It makes no outbound
network requests.

The app stores your volume, haptic feedback, and control sensitivity preferences
on your device. These settings are not sent to the developer. Audio is generated
on your device. The app does not use your geographic location.
