# Oddworks Phase 1 Acceptance — Liminal

## Objective

Prove that Liminal’s authored promise survives a real Release build:

> Enter an unexplained space, discover how movement changes light and sound, then deliberately satisfy the hidden rule to leave.

The first gate is the Doppler space on a supported iPhone or iPad. The remaining six spaces stay in the build, but do not count as release evidence until the opening relationship is comprehensible, accessible, stable, and memorable without coaching.

## Scope ceiling

- Preserve the seven authored spaces, linear progression, offline/local-first design, and no-HUD play surface.
- Do not add levels, narrative, a hub, progression systems, networking, analytics, third-party packages, or live services.
- Settings and system accessibility surfaces are exempt from the no-HUD play constraint.
- Use SceneKit shader modifiers; do not replace SceneKit’s lighting pass with `SCNProgram`.

## Machine-verifiable gate

- The actual Xcode project builds and all rule/data tests pass on a current iOS Simulator.
- Release configuration contains audible, redistribution-safe sound; missing optional stems cannot silently produce an empty audio graph.
- Volume and sensitivity settings apply immediately and persist, including a legitimate zero-volume selection.
- Every space JSON decodes and maps to a rule, shader modifier, geometry, audio configuration, and exit condition.
- An unsigned device Release bundle has the expected executable, privacy manifest, resources, version, and bundle identifier; a signed archive remains a publication gate.
- Release code contains no debug overlay or debug-only procedural behavior required for play.
- App launch, background/foreground audio recovery, interruption recovery, settings access, reduced-audio play, and final-space ending behavior are exercised.

## Accessibility gate

- VoiceOver can identify and operate every settings control.
- Settings are reachable through a discoverable pre-play/system action; a secret gesture alone is not sufficient.
- The opening rule is communicated by both visual and audio change. Neither channel alone is required to discover or complete it.
- Haptics remain optional and off by default.
- Motion sensitivity and physical discomfort are recorded during playtests; a repeated blocker fails the gate.

## Human evidence gate

Eight naive external players receive only the standard prompt: “Play until you reach an ending or choose to stop; I will help only with technical failures.”

Expansion requires:

- 6/8 identify that movement speed changes the environment within five minutes;
- 5/8 deliberately vary speed to test that relationship;
- 4/8 complete Space 1 without coaching;
- no repeated accessibility or motion-sickness blocker.

After 48–72 hours, require at least 5/8 accurate explanations of the movement/environment relationship and 4/8 specific authored-moment recalls. One focused revision and a fresh cohort are allowed. Repeated failure returns the project to bounded redesign or parking.

## Publication gate

Physical-device Metal validation, performance evidence, signed archive/export, accurate screenshots, live support/privacy URLs, pricing, App Store Connect record, external TestFlight evidence, and explicit submission approval are required before App Store publication. Existing “submission ready” prose is not evidence for any of those items.
